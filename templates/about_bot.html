About our bot

You are probably here because you saw LuxuryLeadsAI in your web server's logs.

What it is

Luxury Leads AI builds a chat assistant for estate agencies. Before we introduce ourselves to an agency, we open their website once to check it exists and to understand what they do, so that anything we send is about them rather than a form letter.

What it does, and does not do

  • It reads a small number of pages, no more than one request per second, and never more than a couple of megabytes.
  • It obeys robots.txt. If your site asks robots not to read a page, we do not read it.
  • It does not fill in forms, create accounts, or sign up for anything.
  • It never works around a CAPTCHA or a human check. If it meets one, it stops and the site is left for a person to look at.
  • It collects business contact details that are published on the site - the kind of thing printed on a business card. It does not try to collect anything about private individuals.

How to make it stop

Either of these works, and we would rather you used the first:

  • Email us at {{ support_email }} with your domain and we will add it to our do-not-contact list. That is permanent and covers email as well as the fetcher.
  • Or add this to your robots.txt:
    User-agent: LuxuryLeadsAI
    Disallow: /

Data we hold

If you would like to know what we hold about your business, or want it deleted, write to {{ support_email }} and we will answer. Our privacy policy has the detail.
