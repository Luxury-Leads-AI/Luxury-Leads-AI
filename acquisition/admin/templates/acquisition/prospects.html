Find agencies automatically

Pick a city and a source, and the engine goes looking. Every website it finds is opened once to check the agency is real before it lands here, so a search that invents a name produces nothing.

OpenStreetMap is free and finds agencies that volunteers have mapped. The paid search costs about $0.011 a run and finds agencies with a web presence. Switch sources on or off in Settings. After queueing, press Run the queue on Today.
